My story

From mathematics
to markup

For the majority of my life, I assumed I was destined to be a mathematician, with website development serving as a side gig and favourite interest. I was two years into an MSc degree when I realised I had everything backwards.

Since 2020 I have been focused solely on WordPress development, and I have also built a number of Shopify stores. What started as an interest became a craft: understanding how a page should be structured, why one checkout converts and another does not, and how to make a site fast enough that people actually stay on it.

Along the way I have worked with clients across Pakistan and abroad — retail stores, service businesses, travel operators, educators and agencies. Some wanted a five-page brochure site, others needed a 30-product WooCommerce store with local payment gateways. The constant is the same: hand over something the client can run themselves.

I am interested in the internet’s limitless possibilities for creation, communication and change — and I have been ever since I first got my hands on one.

FAQ

About working together

Everything clients usually ask before we start. If your question isn’t here, send it over on WhatsApp.

Ask a question
How long does a website take?
A 5-page business site is usually ready in 1–2 weeks. A WooCommerce or Shopify store with 30 products typically takes 2–3 weeks depending on how quickly content and product photos arrive.
What do you need from me to start?
Your logo, brand colours if you have them, the text for each page, product images and prices, and access to your domain or hosting. If you do not have content ready, I can help structure it during the discovery call.
Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?
Yes. I work remotely with clients in several countries and run everything over WhatsApp, email and scheduled calls, so timezones have never been a blocker.
Which payment gateways can you integrate?
For Pakistani stores I set up JazzCash, EasyPaisa, cash on delivery and card payments. For international stores I configure the relevant Shopify Payments, Stripe or PayPal options for your market.
Will I be able to edit the website myself?
That is the point of building on WordPress and Shopify. I hand over a walkthrough of your admin panel and you can add pages, posts, products and prices without touching code.
What happens after launch?
Every plan includes 30 days of free support for fixes and small changes. After that you can request changes on an hourly or per-task basis, or move to a maintenance arrangement.
Do you help with domain and hosting?
Yes — I guide you on choosing a domain and hosting suited to your traffic and budget, and can handle the DNS, SSL and email setup for you.
