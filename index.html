FAQ

Questions before we start

Everything clients usually ask before we start. If your question isn’t here, send it over on WhatsApp.

Ask a question
How long does a website take?
A 5-page business site is usually ready in 1–2 weeks. A WooCommerce or Shopify store with 30 products typically takes 2–3 weeks depending on how quickly content and product photos arrive.
What do you need from me to start?
Your logo, brand colours if you have them, the text for each page, product images and prices, and access to your domain or hosting. If you do not have content ready, I can help structure it during the discovery call.
Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?
Yes. I work remotely with clients in several countries and run everything over WhatsApp, email and scheduled calls, so timezones have never been a blocker.
Which payment gateways can you integrate?
For Pakistani stores I set up JazzCash, EasyPaisa, cash on delivery and card payments. For international stores I configure the relevant Shopify Payments, Stripe or PayPal options for your market.
Will I be able to edit the website myself?
That is the point of building on WordPress and Shopify. I hand over a walkthrough of your admin panel and you can add pages, posts, products and prices without touching code.
